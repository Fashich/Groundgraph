# Privacy Policy

version: 2026-10-01
updated: Oct 1, 2026
read: 3 min read

Groundgraph is wallet-only. We never ask for your name, email address or a password.

## What we read
Your public wallet address and public Solana transaction history, which anyone can already see on-chain. If you paste an address to browse in view-only mode, we read that address in the same way.

## What we store
Your wallet address, the time and version of your terms acceptance, your trading limit settings, your watchlist, and the country derived from your IP address for the geofence log. We do not store IP addresses beyond what the infrastructure needs to apply rate limits.

## How wallets are scored
We build a graph from public transactions and score wallets on features such as entry timing, hit rate and links to large wallets. Scores describe on-chain behaviour, not people, and every score shows the parts that produced it.

## Stored in your browser
Your browser keeps a session cookie and the name of the wallet you last used so it can reconnect. Clearing site data removes them.

## Analytics
We do not run advertising trackers and we never sell data.

## Dispute a flag
If you believe your wallet is wrongly flagged, or you want its label hidden from the Radar, send a dispute from that wallet and we will review it. A wallet with a hidden label still trades normally.

## Your choices
Disconnect at any time from the wallet menu. You can ask for the data tied to your address to be deleted by sending a dispute message from that wallet.
