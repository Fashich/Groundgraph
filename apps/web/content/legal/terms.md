## Eligibility
You must be 18 or older and must not be located in, or a resident of, a jurisdiction where price markets or crypto derivatives are restricted, including the United States and sanctioned regions. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you.

## Non-custodial
Groundgraph never holds your funds or private keys. Every trade, market creation and redemption is a transaction that you sign in your own wallet. We cannot reverse, cancel or recover a transaction once it is confirmed.

## Markets and resolution
Each market has two outcomes, ABOVE or BELOW a price level. A share of the winning side settles at $1 and a share of the other side settles at $0, using the Pyth price feed and the rule shown on the market page. The question, rule and oracle are fixed when the market is created, and resolution is final.

## Signals are not advice
Smart money scores, leans and wallet flags are statistical estimates computed from public on-chain data. They can be wrong, late or manipulated. Nothing on Groundgraph is financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

## Fees
Each trade pays a fee set by the market creator (0.5%, 1% or 2%, plus an optional dynamic component capped at 3%) to liquidity providers, plus Solana network fees. Fees are shown before you sign.

## Trading limits
You can set a daily limit, take a break of 1, 7 or 30 days, and turn on session reminders from the Positions page. A break can be extended but never shortened once it has started. Limits apply to buying and creating markets; selling, merging and redeeming stay available.
